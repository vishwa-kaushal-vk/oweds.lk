STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES

Supporting the Development of the Offshore Wind Ecosystem

The company will pursue the following strategic objectives.

Develop an International–Local Partnership Platform

Establish strategic partnerships with experienced offshore wind developers, engineering companies, technology providers, investors and specialist service providers from leading offshore wind markets.

Build Sri Lankan Technical Capacity

Develop a national pool of engineers, environmental specialists, maritime professionals, project managers, researchers, economists, social specialists and skilled technicians capable of supporting offshore wind projects.

Provide Integrated Offshore Wind Services

Offer a coordinated portfolio of technical, professional, commercial and project-support services across the offshore wind project lifecycle.

Promote Technology and Knowledge Transfer

Facilitate the transfer of modern offshore wind technologies, international standards, project-management systems and operational knowledge to Sri Lankan institutions and professionals.

Develop a Local Offshore Wind Supply Chain

Create opportunities for Sri Lankan SMEs, manufacturers, maritime-service providers, logistics companies, construction firms and other businesses to participate in the offshore wind value chain.

Support Investment and Project Development

Assist international and local investors in identifying opportunities, assessing project feasibility, navigating the local operating environment and developing commercially viable offshore wind projects.

Create Green Jobs and Skills

Promote employment, professional development and specialised technical training linked to the emerging offshore wind industry.

Establish a Regional Presence

Over time, expand services beyond Sri Lanka to selected South Asian and Indian Ocean markets, positioning Sri Lanka as a regional knowledge and service hub for offshore renewable energy.
