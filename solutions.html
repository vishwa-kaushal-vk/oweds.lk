FOWEPSL

First Offshore Wind Energy Project of Sri Lanka (FOWEPSL)

A proposed 1.2 GW offshore wind farm

The purpose of this section is to outline the establishment of a 1.2 GW Offshore Wind Farm along the north-western shoreline of Sri Lanka, marking the beginning of the First Offshore Wind Energy Project of Sri Lanka (FOWEPSL).

This pioneering development will generate renewable electricity offshore and transmit it to an onshore substation via HVAC export cables, supplying clean power to the National Grid.

In time, this same infrastructure will also support the proposed Green Hydrogen Production Plant, enabling Sri Lanka to enter the next phase of its energy transition.

According to the World Bank Group’s Energy Sector Management Assistance Program (ESMAP), Sri Lanka possesses an estimated 92 GW of technical offshore wind potential within 200 km of its coastline — 55 GW from fixed-bottom turbines and 37 GW from floating technologies.

With such abundant resources, Sri Lanka can confidently set an ambitious national target: 15 GW of offshore wind power generation by 2050, positioning the country as a regional leader in clean, ocean-based energy.
