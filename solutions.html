Services & Strategic Objectives

Comprehensive professional, technical, and project-development support.

Core Service Offerings

Our services cover areas such as resource assessment, pre-feasibility and feasibility studies, environmental and social assessments, marine and geotechnical studies, project planning, engineering coordination, procurement support, logistics, port and maritime services, construction support, operations and maintenance, skills development, and monitoring and evaluation.

1. International-Local Platform

Establish strategic partnerships with experienced offshore wind developers, engineering companies, technology providers, investors and specialist service providers.

2. Build Technical Capacity

Develop a national pool of engineers, environmental specialists, maritime professionals, project managers, and skilled technicians.

3. Integrated Services

Offer a coordinated portfolio of technical, professional, commercial and project-support services across the offshore wind project lifecycle.

4. Knowledge Transfer

Facilitate the transfer of modern offshore wind technologies, international standards, and operational knowledge to Sri Lankan institutions.

5. Supply Chain Development

Create opportunities for Sri Lankan SMEs, manufacturers, maritime-service providers, logistics companies, and construction firms.

6. Support Investment

Assist international and local investors in identifying opportunities, assessing project feasibility, and navigating the local operating environment.
