About Our Company

Connecting global technology and investment with Sri Lankan capabilities.

Our Vision

"To become Sri Lanka's leading offshore wind energy services platform, connecting global technology, investment and expertise with Sri Lankan capabilities to build a competitive, sustainable and locally rooted offshore renewable-energy industry."

Our Mission

"To mobilise international offshore wind expertise and technology, strengthen Sri Lankan professional and technical capacity, and provide high-quality, commercially viable and environmentally responsible services throughout the offshore wind project lifecycle."

Developing a Local Value Chain

Beyond providing commercial services, the company will aim to contribute to the development of a Sri Lankan offshore wind value chain.

The company will seek to become a trusted partner for international investors, offshore wind developers, technology providers, government agencies, financial institutions and local businesses.

Our Mission Execution

  • Facilitating international-Sri Lankan partnerships.
  • Mobilising international technology in line with the 5IR.
  • Supporting local employment, entrepreneurship and SME participation.
  • Building partnerships with national and international universities and research institutions.
