ABOUT US

Offshore Wind Energy Development Services (Pvt) Ltd

Connecting global technology, investment and expertise with Sri Lankan capabilities.

OUR ROLE

A Sri Lanka-based offshore wind energy services platform

The proposed company will serve as a bridge between international offshore-wind technology, investment and project-development expertise and Sri Lanka's growing local capabilities.

The company will initially focus on providing professional, technical, project-development and support services for offshore wind projects in Sri Lanka and, where appropriate, in other South Asian and Indian Ocean markets.

International Expertise

International collaborators can contribute specialised offshore-wind technologies, global experience, technical standards, project-development methodologies and access to international investors and technology providers.

Sri Lankan Capabilities

Sri Lankan experts, institutions and professionals can contribute knowledge of the country's energy sector, regulatory environment, marine conditions, ports, logistics, environmental and social context, engineering capabilities, labour market and local business ecosystem.

Long-Term Knowledge Base

The company aims to develop a domestic knowledge and service base capable of supporting the offshore wind industry over the longer term.

OUR VISION

Vision

“To become Sri Lanka's leading offshore wind energy services platform, connecting global technology, investment and expertise with Sri Lankan capabilities to build a competitive, sustainable and locally rooted offshore renewable-energy industry.”

OUR MISSION

Mission

“To mobilise international offshore wind expertise and technology, strengthen Sri Lankan professional and technical capacity, and provide high-quality, commercially viable and environmentally responsible services throughout the offshore wind project lifecycle.”
