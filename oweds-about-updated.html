About Us

Our Aim

A resilient, clean-energy future

To lead Sri Lanka’s transition into a resilient, clean-energy future by delivering world-class offshore wind solutions through global collaboration and the strength of our local talent. We envision an energy landscape where international expertise and Sri Lankan ingenuity work side by side—harnessing our island’s vast ocean resources, empowering local communities, and building the capabilities needed to meet the nation’s growing energy demands. Our commitment is to develop sustainable offshore wind infrastructure that elevates national capacity, attracts foreign partnerships, and ensures reliable, affordable, and environmentally responsible energy for generations to come.

Our Approach

Global expertise with local talent

To deliver world-class offshore wind energy services that unite global expertise with Sri Lanka’s own skilled workforce and natural resources. Our mission is to build a sustainable clean-energy future by developing reliable offshore wind infrastructure, empowering local talent, and fostering strategic international partnerships. We are committed to advancing national energy security, accelerating the transition to renewables, and ensuring that Sri Lanka meets its future clean-energy needs with innovation, resilience, and pride.

"Join the Experts Powering Sri Lanka's First Offshore Wind Energy Project"

As a Freelance Consultant Contribute to the Future of Renewable Energy in Sri Lanka

Offshore Wind Energy Development Services (OWEDS) is pleased to invite expressions of interest from qualified professionals who wish to contribute to Sri Lanka's First Offshore Wind Energy Project.

As offshore wind energy emerges as a key component of the global clean energy transition, Sri Lanka has a unique opportunity to harness its vast offshore wind resources to strengthen energy security, drive sustainable economic growth, and support national decarbonisation goals.

To support this pioneering initiative, OWEDS is expanding its network of freelance consultants and subject matter experts from Sri Lanka and around the world.

01

Who We Are Looking For

We welcome applications from professionals and academics with expertise in areas including, but not limited to:

  • Offshore Wind Development
  • Renewable Energy Systems
  • Electrical and Power Engineering
  • Marine and Civil Engineering
  • Environmental and Social Impact Assessment
  • GIS and Spatial Planning
  • Maritime and Port Operations
  • Project Management
  • Energy Policy and Regulation
  • Finance and Investment
  • Research and Innovation
  • Climate and Sustainability
02

Why Join OWEDS?

By joining our consultant network, you will have the opportunity to:

  • Register in our database as a Freelance Consultant
  • Your LinkedIn profile will be linked from our web page for client’s access
  • Contribute to a landmark national renewable energy initiative.
  • Collaborate with local and international industry experts.
  • Support the advancement of offshore wind knowledge and capacity building in Sri Lanka.
  • Participate in multidisciplinary projects that help shape the country's clean energy future.
03

How to Apply

Interested candidates are invited to send an email to admin@oweds.lk with the following information:

  • Full Name
  • Profession
  • Academic Achievements
  • LinkedIn Profile Link
  • WhatsApp Number
  • Email Address
  • Country of Residence
  • Areas of Expertise
