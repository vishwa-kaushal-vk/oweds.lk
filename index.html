First Offshore Wind Energy Project of Sri Lanka (FOWEPSL)

Collaborating with global offshore wind energy innovators, we are working to establish the First Offshore Wind Energy Project of Sri Lanka (FOWEPSL). As an island surrounded by vast open ocean and rich wind resources, Sri Lanka is uniquely positioned to harness offshore wind energy. This initiative aims to strengthen national investment capacity, expand project-development expertise, and build on the country’s rapidly growing local capabilities.

Offshore wind energy must be embraced as a core element of Sri Lanka’s long-term energy strategy, alongside other renewable and conventional sources. Its value extends far beyond power generation, contributing to:

Offshore Wind Energy

Key Contributions

Energy security through diversified and resilient supply.

Cost-effective electricity for households and industries.

Local job creation and broad economic growth.

Climate and environmental benefits that support national sustainability goals.

Grid stability, supported by storage, conversion, and balancing technologies.

Foreign investment attraction, positioning Sri Lanka as a regional clean-energy leader.

The initiation of Green Hydrogen production, unlocking new pathways for energy transition and industrial innovation.
